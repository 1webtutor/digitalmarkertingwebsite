Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Official Domain: vyoradigital.com

1. Introduction & Scope

Vyora Digital (“we”, “us”, “our”, or “Vyora”), operating under the domain vyoradigital.com, is committed to safeguarding the privacy and personal data of our website visitors, clients, and partners. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, utilize, process, store, and disclose information in accordance with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and other relevant global data protection regulations.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you directly provide to us as well as data gathered automatically when interacting with our website and digital assets:

  • Directly Provided Information: When you complete our contact forms, audit reservation forms, or communicate via WhatsApp/phone, we collect your name, business email address, phone/WhatsApp number, company name, website URL, and details regarding your marketing goals and advertising budget.
  • Technical & Usage Information: We automatically collect device information, IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited, time spent on pages, referral sources, and conversion milestones through secure server logs and first-party cookies.
  • Server-Side Conversion Data (CAPI): In delivering performance marketing services, we process server-to-server conversion events (such as leads, appointment bookings, and purchases) to ensure attribution accuracy.

3. How We Use Your Information

We utilize collected data strictly for legitimate business and client service purposes:

  • To schedule, prepare, and deliver your requested 30-minute growth diagnostic session.
  • To manage client campaigns, produce creative assets, and build custom sub-second landing page funnels.
  • To send operational updates, campaign performance metrics, and emergency notifications via WhatsApp and email.
  • To prevent fraudulent activity, safeguard infrastructure security, and ensure regulatory compliance.

4. Zero Data Selling Guarantee & Third-Party Processors

Vyora Digital will never sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal or business data to any third party under any circumstances. We share data exclusively with trusted enterprise infrastructure providers required to deliver our services, including Meta Platforms (Conversions API), Google LLC (Analytics and Ad delivery), WhatsApp Cloud API, and Cloudflare Pages (secure edge hosting). All processors are bound by strict contractual confidentiality and data security obligations.

5. Data Security & Encryption

We enforce enterprise-grade security protocols, including 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit, strict role-based access controls, encrypted databases, and multi-factor authentication (MFA) across all agency tools and dashboards.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to request access to your personal data, request correction of inaccurate data, withdraw previously granted consent, or request the complete deletion of your records from our systems. To exercise any of these rights, please email us directly at privacy@vyoradigital.com or hello@vyoradigital.com.
