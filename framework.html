The Vyora 4-Step Revenue Architecture

We do not rely on guesswork, trendy marketing hacks, or algorithm luck. We deploy a repeatable, data-backed 4-phase framework designed to take brands from unpredictable sales spikes to consistent, highly profitable customer acquisition.

Phase 01 · Days 1–7
Discovery & Unit Economics Audit

Diagnostic Teardown: Finding the Silent Leaks

Before a single rupee of ad spend is deployed, we perform a forensic examination of your historical ad accounts, conversion data, customer economics, and competitor playbooks. We map out exactly where prospective clients are dropping off.

Pixel & CAPI Health Check

Verify event deduplication, server-side attribution accuracy, and Event Quality Score across Meta & Google.

Unit Economics Modeling

Calculate your target Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Lifetime Value (LTV), and allowable ad budget thresholds.

Competitor Ad Teardown

Analyze your top 5 competitors’ active ads, creative hooks, landing page promises, and price positioning.

Phase 02 · Days 8–14
Architecture & High-Converting Assets

Building the Sub-Second Funnel & Creative Arsenal

We engineer the infrastructure that turns high-cost traffic into qualified revenue. We write, design, and code lightning-fast landing pages while scripting and producing high-converting video and static creatives.

Sub-Second Landing Pages

Custom coded, zero-bloat landing pages loading in under 0.8 seconds, optimized for mobile conversion rates.

15 Creative Iterations

Short-form vertical video ads, founder VSLs, and high-CTR carousels engineered around 3-second psychological hooks.

Instant WhatsApp CRM Sync

Webhooks deliver new leads instantly into WhatsApp and your CRM within 15 seconds with automated follow-ups.

Phase 03 · Days 15–35
The 21-Day Calibration Blitz

Scientific Traffic Launch & Algorithmic Optimization

We launch targeted media buying across Meta Ads and Google Search with controlled testing budgets. We stress-test hooks, audiences, and offers to determine the single lowest-cost pathway to qualified customers.

Creative Testing Matrix

Systematically isolate hook variations, body copy, and CTAs to discover the 20% of creatives delivering 80% of sales.

Intent Search Domination

Capture high-intent in-market buyers on Google with rigorous negative keyword shielding and top-of-page bidding.

Lead Screening QA

Audit inbound lead quality daily with your sales team to filter out spam and verify buyer budget qualification.

Phase 04 · Days 36+
Predictable Scaling & Automation

Unlocking Aggressive Scale Without Margin Decay

Once unit economics and conversion rates are proven, we push ad spend aggressively. We introduce horizontal audience scaling, continuous creative replenishment, and omnichannel remarketing loops.

Budget Compounding

Scale ad spend by 20% to 50% weekly on verified winning ad sets without tripping algorithm reset penalties.

Creative Fatigue Shield

Deploy fresh creative angles every 10 days to keep frequency low, CTR high, and cost per acquisition steady.

Omnichannel Retargeting
