Why We Exist

The Traditional Agency Model is Fundamentally Broken

Most business owners have been burned by digital marketing agencies. The story is always the same: you get pitched by charming senior directors, but the moment you sign a 6-month contract, your account is handed off to an overworked junior intern managing 20 accounts at once.

At the end of every month, you receive a glossy 40-page PDF filled with vanity metrics: “impressions went up 300%” or “reach doubled”—while your cash register is empty, your leads are cold, and your actual sales pipeline is flat.

Vyora Digital was built to invert this broken model. We do not track impressions or congratulate ourselves on clicks. We measure our worth solely on Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), and Net Booked Revenue.
