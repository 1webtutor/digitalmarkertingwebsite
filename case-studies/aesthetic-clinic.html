1. The Challenge: Fierce Local Competition & Slow Front-Desk Response

Novaderm Aesthetic Clinic is a premier cosmetic dermatology practice offering advanced skin rejuvenation, laser treatments, and hair restoration. Despite having world-class doctors and state-of-the-art US-FDA approved lasers, the clinic struggled with patient acquisition.

Their Google Business Profile sat on page 2 of local search results, while large corporate hospital chains dominated the map pack. Furthermore, patients searching high-intent terms like "dermatologist clinic near me" or "laser skin rejuvenation treatment" were captured by competing clinics running Google Search Ads, while Novaderm's inbound leads waited 3 to 4 hours for front-desk replies.

2. The Solution: High-Intent Google Search Ads, Maps 3-Pack & WhatsApp AI

Vyora Digital engineered an end-to-end Google search acquisition pipeline:

Google Search Ads (Exact & Phrase Match)

Targeted bottom-of-funnel commercial keywords like [dermatologist clinic near me] and "laser skin rejuvenation treatment" with 9.4/10 Quality Scores and strict negative keyword lists to eliminate junk clicks.

Google Maps 3-Pack Authority Engine

Standardized NAP citations across 60+ healthcare directories, injected MedicalBusiness schema markup, geo-tagged clinic photos, and automated 5-star review collection after appointments.

Call Extensions & Promoted Map Pins

Direct click-to-call mobile buttons and promoted location pins generated 620+ calls and 840+ driving direction requests directly from local search results.

24/7 WhatsApp AI Appointment Concierge

Deployed a custom WhatsApp bot that answered treatment FAQs, pre-screened patient skin concerns in < 45 seconds, and booked consultation slots directly into the doctor's calendar.

3. The Results: 1,110 Total Bookings, 185+ / Mo at -62.4% CAC

Within 6 months of campaign execution, Novaderm experienced a transformative surge in booked patients:

  • ✓ Achieved #1 position on Google Maps for 14 major dermatology and cosmetic treatment keywords.
  • ✓ Scaled to 185+ monthly qualified patient consultations (1,110 total conversions across the 6-month cycle).
  • ✓ Cost per acquisition (CAC) dropped by 62.4%, from ₹628 down to ₹236.70 per booked patient consultation.
