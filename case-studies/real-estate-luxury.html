1. The Challenge: High CPL & Unqualified Lead Leakage

Aura Luxury Villas had launched an exclusive enclave of bespoke private villas priced between ₹2.8 Cr and ₹4.5 Cr. Prior to partnering with Vyora Digital, their previous marketing agency was running generic Facebook Lead Ads pointing to an unoptimized 5.2-second slow WordPress website.

Their sales team was overwhelmed by low-intent tire-kickers, students asking for price lists, and disconnected phone numbers. Cost per genuine site visit was skyrocketing above ₹4,200, and inventory sales had ground to a halt.

2. The Architecture: Sub-Second Microsite & WhatsApp Qualification

Vyora Digital dismantled the old marketing approach and deployed a 3-part growth architecture within 18 days:

0.65s Luxury Architectural Microsite

Rebuilt the landing page from scratch with zero plugins, interactive 3D floorplans, and mobile-optimized video walkthroughs that loaded in under 650 milliseconds.

HNI & NRI Meta Ad Targeting

Crafted cinematic short-form video hooks showcasing architectural craftsmanship, paired with Meta CAPI server tracking and high-income postal code exclusions.

Instant WhatsApp Booking Concierge

Integrated a 2-step WhatsApp chatbot that greeted enquiries in < 30 seconds, qualified buyer purchase timelines, and confirmed private site tours directly.

Live Google Sheets & CRM Sync

Instant SMS & WhatsApp alerts sent to Aura's senior relationship managers with verified buyer notes and scheduled site visit calendar times.

3. The Results: ₹14.8 Cr Sold in 90 Days

Within 90 days of launching the new growth funnel:

  • ✓ ₹14.8 Crore in luxury villa units contracted and sold.
  • ✓ Cost per verified site-visit enquiry dropped by 48% (from ₹2,200 to ₹1,140).
  • ✓ Achieved an overall blended 4.2x ROAS across all paid media spend.
