Client Overview & Challenge

Zenith Living is an upscale home decor and ergonomic lifestyle DTC brand catering to design-conscious urban professionals. Despite high customer satisfaction and repeat organic interest, the brand was trapped at a revenue ceiling of ₹4,50,000/month for five consecutive months.

Whenever they scaled ad spend beyond ₹1,00,000/month, Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) doubled, ROAS collapsed from 2.2x to 1.1x, and their Shopify checkout leaked 78% of shoppers at the shipping address step.

The 3 Core Breakage Points Identified in Audit:

  • Creative Fatigue & Static Creatives: They were running only 3 static product photos. Ad frequency peaked above 4.8 within 10 days, burning out lookalike audiences.
  • Shopify Multi-Page Checkout Friction: 4-step mobile checkout required 14 form fields and loaded in 3.6 seconds, shedding 78% of cart initiations.
  • Zero Post-Abandonment Conversions: Generic email recovery had an open rate of only 11% and generated less than 1.8% recovered revenue.
The Execution Blueprint

How Vyora Built a Predictable 10.6x Growth Engine

01

Creative Velocity Engine (15 UGC Hooks / Week)

We replaced stagnant studio renders with dynamic, short-form creator videos (UGC). Each creative was engineered around the 3-Second Hook Method: addressing unboxing sensory appeal, problem/solution comparisons, and aesthetic interior setups. With 15 variations rotating weekly, ad burnout dropped to zero and click-through rate (CTR) surged from 1.1% to 3.8%.

02

1-Click Fast Checkout Funnel (0.8s LCP)

Rebuilt landing pages with pre-filled OTP authentication, 1-click UPI checkout, and sticky dynamic bundles. Removed 9 extraneous input fields. Checkout abandonment plummeted by 32% within 72 hours of deployment.

03

Instant 3-Step WhatsApp Abandonment Rescue

Connected our Meta Conversions API (CAPI) and WhatsApp Cloud API. When a user abandoned their cart, an automated WhatsApp message was triggered at Minute 15 offering a 1-tap checkout link and limited-time free express delivery. WhatsApp open rates exceeded 94%, recovering 24.2% of abandoned purchases on autopilot.

04

Targeting with Meta Advantage+ & First-Party Data

Fed verified server-side purchaser data into Meta ASC (Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns) alongside high-intent Google Search Shopping ads. Algorithm efficiency improved radically, allowing us to scale daily ad budget from ₹4,000 to ₹35,000 while maintaining a 4.8x blended ROAS.
