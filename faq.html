🚀 Onboarding & Execution Velocity

How fast do we launch after signing? +

Our sprint velocity takes you from kickoff to live ad campaigns in 7 to 10 business days. During this window, we audit tracking, build your sub-second landing pages, produce your initial batch of video/static creatives, and wire up instant CRM and WhatsApp notifications.

What access or credentials will you need from us? +

We require Partner-level access in Meta Business Manager and Google Ads (you never share passwords), DNS CNAME access to host your custom landing pages on a subdomain (e.g. get.yourbrand.com), and access to your CRM or WhatsApp Business number for webhook sync.

Will we be dealing with junior account managers? +

Never. At Vyora, we do not employ junior account reps who hide behind automated emails. You communicate directly with Senior Growth Partners, Media Buyers, and Funnel Technologists through a dedicated WhatsApp or Slack channel.

💰 Ad Spend & Media Budgets

What is the minimum recommended monthly ad spend? +

For our Starter Accelerator tier, we recommend a minimum ad budget of ₹30,000 to ₹50,000/month so the algorithms have enough data to exit the learning phase. For the Growth Engine tier, brands typically spend between ₹1,00,000 to ₹5,00,000+/month.

How is our media spend paid? +

You pay Meta and Google directly using your own corporate credit card or debit method. Vyora only charges our flat monthly management retainer. This gives you 100% financial clarity, receipt visibility for taxes, and credit card rewards.

🎯 Attribution & Conversion Tracking

How do you handle iOS 14.5 and ad-blockers? +

We deploy server-side Meta Conversions API (CAPI) and Google Tag Manager Server Containers. Events are sent directly from our Cloudflare server infrastructure directly to Meta and Google's endpoints, bypassing client-side browser blockers and restoring full attribution fidelity.

How do we verify lead quality? +

Every lead captured through our funnels is pre-screened with mandatory qualification questions (e.g. investment budget, timeline, service requirement). Leads are delivered with full UTM parameters, and we hold bi-weekly reviews with your sales team to optimize for closed revenue.

🛡️ Ownership & Partnership Terms

Who owns the creatives, funnels, and ad accounts? +

You do. 100%. All ad accounts, pixel data, custom landing page source code, and video masters are your intellectual property. If our partnership ever concludes, everything stays in your control with zero fees or hostages.

Can we cancel at any time? +

Yes. We do not enforce 6-month or 12-month lock-in contracts. Our retainers operate on a month-to-month basis with a simple 30-day notice. We earn your business every month through demonstrable revenue results.
