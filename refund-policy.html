Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Official Domain: vyoradigital.com

1. Professional Retainer Fees

Vyora Digital provides bespoke marketing engineering, video production, web architecture, and media management services. Our monthly retainer fees cover dedicated senior engineering hours, creative production, server infrastructure, and proactive media optimization. All retainers are billed in advance for each 30-day billing cycle.

2. 30-Day Cancellation Freedom

We do not enforce 6-month or 12-month lock-in contracts. Clients may cancel or pause their monthly retainer at any time by giving a 30-day written notice to billing@vyoradigital.com or via their dedicated Slack/WhatsApp executive channel. Services will continue uninterrupted through the end of the paid billing period.

3. 7-Day Onboarding Grace Period

For new client onboarding, if you decide to cancel within 7 calendar days of initial contract signing and invoice payment—and before any custom landing pages or creative assets have been finalized and delivered—Vyora Digital will refund your monthly retainer fee minus a standard administrative setup and audit fee of ₹10,000.

4. Ongoing Monthly Retainers

Once work has commenced for an active billing cycle (funnels built, creatives produced, campaigns managed), monthly retainers are non-refundable for that active cycle. This is because time, agency personnel, and technical resources are already fully committed.

5. Third-Party Advertising Media Spend

All ad spend is paid directly by the client to advertising platforms (Meta Platforms, Google Ads, etc.). Vyora Digital does not hold, mark up, or process ad spend capital. Any inquiries regarding ad spend refunds must be directed to Meta or Google directly in accordance with their respective advertiser policies.
